打印中心请注意：

- 三个 STL 各打印 1 个。
- 材料优先 PETG，PLA 也可以做样机。
- 0.2mm 层高，3 道墙，15%-25% 填充。
- 按模型自动加支撑。
- 这是电子设备外壳样机，屏幕窗口和顶部麦克风孔请尽量清理干净。

Project:
Desktop robot enclosure for Orange Pi AI Pro 20T official case, 5 inch HDMI touchscreen,
M260C six-mic ring module, M260C adapter board, and 40 mm round speaker.

Known screen dimensions used:
- 5 inch HDMI touchscreen
- Resolution: 800 x 480
- Active display area: 108.8 x 65.3 mm
- PCB size: 121.11 x 77.93 mm
- Screw hole spacing was not confirmed from the product images. The model uses
  a loose pocket/retainer-lip approach for the first prototype.

Recommended print settings:
- Material: PETG preferred, PLA acceptable for first prototype
- Nozzle: 0.4 mm
- Layer height: 0.2 mm
- Wall/perimeter: 3 or more
- Infill: 15% to 25%
- Support: enable support where needed, especially screen opening, ears/speaker pods,
  rear service opening, and overhangs
- Unit: millimeter
- Quantity: 1 each

Notes:
- This is an engineering prototype. Please do not scale the STL files.
- Parts are designed as front shell, back shell, and base/body for easier assembly and maintenance.
- Please keep the screen window and top microphone holes clean after support removal.
- Light sanding or minor hole cleanup may be needed after printing.
